THE ROWS ARE REAL: they are the actual output of the grounding check function run on those five candidate rewrites, not mock-ups. The check rejects any new number, including number words and non-ASCII digits, and any content word that is not in the original line apart from a short list of replacement verbs. It also requires a rewrite to be a single line that replaces one whole CV line. WHAT ELSE IS CHECKED: quotes must appear verbatim inside one bullet, so they cannot span two. Summaries and explanations are checked for invented numbers, names and links. Email sentences must each be listed as a claim that restates its quote. All angle brackets in untrusted text are neutralised so a CV cannot close a data tag or inject instructions. These checks were hardened after an independent review found ways around an earlier, weaker version. HONEST LIMIT: the check proves a quote exists in the user’s text. A quote can be a fragment of a longer sentence, and the check does not prove the model drew the right conclusion from it.
