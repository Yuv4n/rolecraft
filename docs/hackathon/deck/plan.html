1:14 to 1:32. About 18 seconds. SAY: "Then a plan sized to the person’s time. At four hours a week for six weeks, there is a 24-hour budget. A data modelling project needs twelve hours and doesn’t fit, so the tool says no and moves it to ‘after you apply’. Move the slider to eight hours and everything fits." THE REAL NUMBERS ON SCREEN: at 4 hours a week the budget is 24 hours, 4 hours are held back for the tailored CV and outreach, and 10 hours are scheduled across 2 tasks. At 8 hours a week the budget is 48 hours, 7 are held back and 22 are scheduled across all 3 tasks. DETAIL: the fitting is plain arithmetic in code, not model output, so the plan updates instantly as the slider moves. Tasks are taken in priority order, and a task that no longer fits is deferred with its reason. A smaller, lower-priority task is still tried after a bigger one is deferred.
