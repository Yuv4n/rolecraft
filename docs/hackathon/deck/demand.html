The figures come from different countries, years and definitions, so they are not directly comparable. They support a general picture of demand. They do not measure demand for Rolecraft specifically, and no usage or market-size claim is made for the product. 1. 43 to 109 applications per internship posting: Handshake Internships Index 2025, reported by CNBC. 43 was 2022–23, 62 was 2023–24 and 109 was 2024–25. The report ties the fall in postings to a correction after post-pandemic overhiring. 2. +27% internship applications and about 8% fewer places: ISE Student Recruitment Survey, 2025–26 cycle, as reported in survey coverage. The search summary labelled this edition as current. Check the ISE report for the exact wording and base. 3. 38% of UK university students uncertain about their plans, and 45% unprepared for getting a job: Prospects press release, "Nearly half of university students unprepared for employment". The year was not confirmed in the search summary, so check it. 4. 53% received career guidance and 38% received timely career information: Strada survey of alumni from the classes of 2020 to 2023, as summarised by Inside Higher Ed in October 2024. ISE also reports that part of the rise in applications is attributed to AI tools and easy-apply features that make mass applications faster.
