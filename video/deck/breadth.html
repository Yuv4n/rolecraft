The ten CVs are the ones in the test suite: a product analyst, a nurse, a teacher, an accountant, a marketer, a plain-text customer service CV, a software engineer, a chef, a trainee solicitor and a psychology graduate. The test also checks that text with no recognisable experience returns no directions instead of a guess. DETAIL: tailoring finds the achievement lines with or without bullet markers, so plain-text and PDF-extracted CVs work too. Honest limit: this is a keyword engine over a fixed role list.
